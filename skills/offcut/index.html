FAQ

Before you ask.

Is the estimate binding?
It is the same math we quote with, but a binding quote follows a ten-minute scoping call — edge cases (e-commerce, multilingual, custom illustration) move the number and we say so first.
Why is the slider capped at 30 pages?
Because larger projects stop being page-counts and become scope conversations. If you need more, write to us with a rough structure and we will estimate it properly.
What counts as a page?
A distinct layout with its own content. Five product variants that share one layout count as one page plus a data change.
Do you take rush jobs?
Sometimes, if the calendar allows — the +$100/page is real and it buys you a dedicated week, not overtime sweat.
